# Liberty. Equality. Data.

> An open ecosystem for Personal AI, built on individual control and data sovereignty.

Personal AI should remain independent of any one model, cloud provider or company. We bring together people, researchers, developers, organizations and infrastructure partners to make that possible on shared and open foundations.

## Six Personal AI freedoms

1. **Choose your AI** — the model serving a person is their choice.
2. **Control your personal data** — what is stored, shared, remembered and forgotten is decided by the person it describes.
3. **Choose where your data lives** — region, infrastructure and legal jurisdiction are choices.
4. **Use local or cloud AI** — a personal layer should work privately on-device or at scale in the cloud.
5. **Switch providers** — leaving should be as practical as joining.
6. **Keep your Personal AI over time** — personal context should outlive technology generations, devices and companies.

## Building the ecosystem

Souvéa is the founding contribution and reference Personal AI platform: a working platform built around personal context, user control and open architecture. It makes the ecosystem's principles available for research, pilots and developer work today.

There is no single way to contribute. We welcome collaboration across research, communities, consumer organizations, trusted infrastructure, privacy and identity, and strategic investment.
